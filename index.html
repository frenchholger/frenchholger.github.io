Support server rules

The rules of our community server. Staff have the final say in decisions.

General conduct
  1. Spamming, flooding, mass reacting and chaining messages is not allowed.
  2. Racism, sexism, slurs and similar content is not allowed.
  3. Harassment, threats, bullying, verbal abuse and similar content is not allowed.
  4. Disruptive content such as crash gifs/videos, being toxic or causing drama is not allowed.
  5. Pinging anyone in a message or reply without a valid reason is not allowed, unless you have the permitted role.
  6. The server language is English, so you must speak English.
  7. Encouraging, attempting, aiding in, or falsely accusing others of conduct that breaks the rules or Discord’s ToS is not allowed.
  8. Joking about being underage, or purposely misleading others to believe you are underage, is prohibited.
  9. Advertising is forbidden unless it happens naturally in conversation or a Moderator or above gives permission.
  10. Alternative accounts are not allowed. If we find multiple accounts, the alt is permanently banned and the main account is punished.
Behaviour rules
  1. Abusing permissions, bugs or glitches is not allowed.
  2. Encouraging, attempting, aiding in, or falsely accusing others of conduct that breaks the rules, Discord ToS or Roblox ToS is prohibited.
  3. Evading moderation with alts, glitches or other unintended ways is not allowed.
  4. Participation in illegal behaviour and other unethical conduct is prohibited.
  5. Impersonating another person is prohibited unless it is an obvious parody.
  6. Complaining about an in-game punishment is not allowed. If you were punished unfairly, create a ticket.
  7. Mini-modding is not allowed. Don’t report every small infraction by pinging staff or opening a ticket.
  8. Aim to be respectful and civil towards other members.
  9. Staff have the final say. To appeal a punishment, contact a high rank through the ticket channel.
  10. The rules don’t apply to administrators.

Original writing by Airoslav (@airoslav.). Updated by Izeriko (@izeriko).

Staff applications

Every application goes through five stages. The bot sends you a DM when your application is accepted or denied.

Pending staff reviewWaits in a channel until a staff member starts handling it.
Initial background checkAn automatic check by the bot. It may take a while.
Being consideredYour application was good enough to move to the next step.
Manual background checkAn in-depth manual check, then accepted or sent to staff opinions.
Staff opinionsThe staff team discusses your application.

Staff punishment system

Staff who break the server rules or staff rules are punished from step 1 to 4, unless a case calls for something else. Higher ranks use /punishstaff, and records can be viewed with /punishments.

Verbal WarnA text warning from a higher rank.
Written WarnSaved by the bot, and the member is notified by DM.
Status removal and demotionStaff roles removed for a week, then demotion. A second chance.
Being firedRemoved from the staff team. The member is notified by DM.

Two Verbal Warns merge into one Written Warn. Two Written Warns can lead to status removal or being fired, which most often follows a status removal.

Records are removed automatically after 2 months for warns (Verbal Warns alone do not count toward promotions), 6 months for status removal and demotion, and 1 year 6 months for being fired, after which the member can reapply.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what data French Holger Bot collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the bot you agree to it, together with the Terms of Service above.

1. Data we collect

  • Server settings: server, role and channel IDs, language, shop settings, log channels and application settings.
  • Moderation records: warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans and other actions, with the member ID, moderator ID, reason and time, plus moderator shift and quota statistics.
  • Staff records: staff punishments (type, reason, date) and application records.
  • Economy data: FSP and Baguette balances, shop purchases and trades.
  • Application data: answers you submit, the review status and decisions, and the application blacklist.

We store Discord user IDs, not your email, password, phone number or location. Discord usernames and tags may appear in moderation reasons or logs when staff enter them.

2. Messages

The bot reads messages only to run commands, to run enabled features such as FSP phrase rewards, and, if you submit a staff application, for the automatic background check. That check may scan message history the bot can access and compare it with a word list set by the server’s admins. We do not keep a general archive of server messages.

3. How we use data

We use data only to provide the bot’s features: moderation, the economy, staff applications, shifts and logs. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except as needed to run the service (see section 5).

4. Where data is shown

Moderation records, staff records and application details can be seen by the server staff with the matching permission, and may be posted to the server’s configured log and review channels. Server admins control who has access.

5. Hosting and third parties

The bot runs on Discord’s platform and is stored on our hosting provider’s servers. Discord and the hosting provider process data under their own policies. Discord’s Privacy Policy is at discord.com/privacy.

6. Retention

We keep data while the bot is in the server and the feature needs it. Staff punishment records are removed automatically on the schedule in the staff punishment system above. Staff can also remove warnings and actions. If the bot is removed from a server, you can ask us to delete that server’s data.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may object to its processing. We will answer as soon as we reasonably can, and data may be kept where needed for safety or to enforce server rules. Contact us in the support server. Moderation decisions themselves are made by each server’s staff, so appeals go to that server first.

8. Children

The bot is intended for people who meet Discord’s minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below it, and will delete it if we learn we have.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data, but no online service is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy, and the date above will change when we do. Continuing to use the bot means you accept the updated policy. Questions or requests: join the support server.
